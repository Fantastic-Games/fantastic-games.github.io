Hey there! We are Fantastic Games, a group of high school and college buddies coming together to make games we want to play. Inspired by the many video games we've all played growing up, we figure it was about time to start making our own! 

Our ultimate goal is to **make games we want to play**, without being forced to follow trends or be funneled towards making any one genre. We want to explore what we can do in this medium we've all grown up with. From creating games inspired by our childhood favorites, to trying out ideas left behind, to experimenting with genres yet to be defined, we want do them all! We hope you follow us on our journey as we hit the ground running! We will be announcing Fantastic Games' first project very soon, so please keep an eye out on our devlog.
